# Brief

The default form: a question with numbers in it that no other form fits. Most answers end here,
as final text, with no page.

## Collect

The one to three figures that answer the question, each from the page or file that publishes it,
with that page's date. A figure you work out (a share, a change, a per-person cost) shows its
arithmetic in one line.

## Final text

1. The answer in one sentence, carrying its number.
2. Up to three lines, one reason each, each with its figure and source.
3. What is not solid: an estimate, a source older than the question, two sources that disagree
   (give both).

A page is worth making only when the reasons need a chart or a table longer than five rows; then
the page is these same lines on top, the chart or table under them.

## Example (illustrative numbers)

> Q: How much has the price of a subway ride gone up in this city?
>
> Up 39% in ten years: ₩1,050 in 2015 to ₩1,450 now (city transit site, fares page, 2026).
> - The last rise was ₩1,250 → ₩1,400 in 2023, then ₩1,550 planned for next year was cut to
>   ₩1,450 (city press release, 2025-06).
> - Consumer prices rose 24% over the same years (national statistics office CPI, 2015 = 100 → 124),
>   so the fare grew faster than prices.
> - Not solid: the planned rise for next year is a proposal, not a decision.
