# Price check

Where one exact thing is cheapest to buy right now, and whether now is a good time.

## Collect

- The exact item: model number, size, color, condition (new, refurbished, used). Two listings of
  "the same" thing often differ here; a cheaper one that is not the same item is left out or
  said to differ.
- From each seller: the price the user pays at the door — item, shipping, tax or duty, any fee —
  delivery date, return terms, and the listing's link. Open each listing; a search result's price
  is often stale.
- Price history when a price tracker or comparison site shows it: the lowest and usual price over
  months, so "cheapest now" can be told from "cheap".
- Imports: duty and tax at the user's border, and the exchange rate with its date.

## Final text

Where to buy and the total paid, how much it saves against the next best, and whether the price
is low, usual or high against its recent history. The time you checked.

## Page

Only when there are more than about five sellers or the history matters: a table sorted by total
paid (the pick marked), a bar chart of totals (`--highlight` the pick), the history as a line when
there is one.

## Example (illustrative numbers)

> Cheapest now: Shop B, £389 delivered by Friday — £24 under the next best (Shop A, £413 with
> £3 shipping). The lowest in the last six months was £372 (a comparison site's history,
> 2026-05); £389 is near the low end of its usual £385–420.
> Checked 2026-09-19 14:10.
